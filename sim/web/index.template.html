Camera input

Open Scan QR on the device: main menu 1, or Fn + Q.

Simulator only. The real device reads QR codes with its own camera module; it has no webcam, radio or USB data.

Controls

  • Arrow keys (bottom left) move, Enter selects, Esc goes back
  • 1–6 dice, H/T coin, Del undo
  • Type 4 letters of a word; it completes itself
  • Your computer keyboard works too

Keyboard · M5Stack CardKB v1.1

The layout of the real CardKB v1.1 (50 keys). Sym types the small symbol beside a letter; ⇧ once for a capital, twice for caps lock. Fn + Q opens the QR scanner.

This build · M4

QR input: Scan QR reads SeedQRs, seed words, PSBTs (UR, BBQr, Specter, Base64), keys and addresses. QR output: Export xpub, address QR, Backup SeedQR. Seeds come from dice and coin flips only. TEST ONLY: never use these seeds for real funds.
