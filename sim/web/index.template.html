    Vault · SE050 driver, simulated chip

    No vault

    Settings > Security > PIN makes a vault (M10). The vault runs the real SE050 driver (T=1 over I2C, SCP03, SE05x commands) against a simulated chip; the chip, the device key and the vault flash live in this page's memory only: nothing is written to browser storage, and reloading the page is a new device without a vault. Store a seed in Seeds & keys > Seed vault, then Power > Power off and any key: the lock screen, and after the PIN the seed is loaded again. Stretching the PIN (Argon2id, 8 MiB) takes a moment here, as on the device. Tamper check (Tab on the lock screen): this page's WASM is the firmware image, SHA-256 …; another build gives other words.

    Controls

    • Arrow keys (bottom left) move, Enter selects, Esc goes back, a number opens its row
    • ? explains the screen (F1 where text is typed); any key closes it
    • 1–6 dice, H/T coin, D20: number then Space, Del undo
    • Type 4 letters of a word; it completes itself
    • Your computer keyboard works too

    Keyboard · M5Stack CardKB v1.1

    The layout of the real CardKB v1.1 (50 keys). Sym types the small symbol beside a letter (Sym+K is ?); ⇧ once for a capital, twice for caps lock. Fn + Q opens the QR scanner from any menu.

    This build · M11 (in progress)

    M11 "advanced backup" so far: SLIP-39 Shamir shares — open a SLIP-39 wallet from its shares (Seeds & keys > Load a seed > SLIP-39 shares), make a new one from dice split as 2 of 3, 3 of 5 or custom groups (Seeds & keys > New seed > SLIP-39 wallet), and make another set of shares of a loaded one (its seed options). M10 "secure storage": a vault locked by a two-part PIN with two anti-phishing words, on the SE050 driver (T=1, SCP03) over a simulated chip (Settings > Security). Seeds you store (Seeds & keys > Seed vault; never the passphrase), registered multisig wallets and the settings come back after power off and on. Trick PINs (Settings > Security): a decoy wallet, erase then "wrong PIN", erase then decoy, or look blank — none uses an attempt. Tamper check: Tab on the lock screen shows two words for your check code, this firmware and this device. Without a vault the device works exactly as in M9. M9 "Bitpunk Phosphor": the same functions as M8, every screen redrawn as a phosphor terminal. Menus by task (Sign · Receive & verify · Seeds & keys · Multisig · SD · Settings · Power), a first-run screen, a session log under the main menu, a boot self-test log, a screen saver that hides secrets, the review with a large amount, a ledger and a log of what was verified, a DOS-style file list, error codes, ? help on every screen, and screen effects (Settings). Every output (QR, files, signatures) is byte-for-byte the M8 one. Seeds come from dice and coin flips only. TEST ONLY: never use these seeds for real funds.
