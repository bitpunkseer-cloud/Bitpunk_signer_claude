Controls

  • D-pad moves, ENTER selects, CANCEL goes back
  • 1–6 dice, H/T coin, DEL undo
  • Type 4 letters of a word; it completes itself
  • Your computer keyboard works too (Esc = CANCEL)

Keyboard

No Ctrl, Alt or F-keys. SYM types the orange symbol on a key; SHIFT once for a capital, twice for CAPS. QR opens the scanner.
